OneGLP lets you record GLP-1 symptoms and side-effect notes beside doses, weight, appetite, nutrition and photos. It gives you a private record for review. It does not diagnose symptoms, explain side effects or provide medical advice.

How it works

  1. Log symptoms or side-effect notes when you want a record.
