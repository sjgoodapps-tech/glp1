GLP-1 side effect and symptom tracker

GLPzy lets you record GLP-1 symptoms and side-effect notes beside doses, weight, appetite, nutrition and photos. It gives you a private record for review. It does not diagnose symptoms, explain side effects or provide medical advice.

Symptom tracking works best when it is plain and consistent.

GLPzy keeps symptom notes close to the dose, weight, appetite and nutrition context you already record.

How it works

  1. Log symptoms or side-effect notes when you want a record.
  2. Add appetite, nutrition, dose and weight context without asking the app to interpret it.
  3. Export records or create a clinician-ready PDF summary for appointments if needed.

What GLPzy does

  • records symptoms and appetite notes
  • keeps dose and weight context nearby
  • supports export and appointment summaries

What GLPzy does not do

  • diagnose symptoms
  • explain side effects
  • triage urgent issues
  • replace medical advice
