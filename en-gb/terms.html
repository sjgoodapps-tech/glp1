4. Product boundary

GLPzy does not provide medical advice. Estimated Exposure Trend is a relative personal-tracking estimate. It is not a measured blood concentration. Not medical advice. Do not use Estimated Exposure to guide dosing. Always check with your clinician before making medical decisions.
