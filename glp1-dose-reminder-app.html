Quick answer

GLP-1 dose reminder app

GLPzy helps you keep GLP-1 dose dates, reminder times, last dose history, injection sites and notes in one private iPhone app. It can support your routine record, but it does not change your schedule or tell you when to dose.

Get GLPzy

Use reminder records to reduce scattered calendar notes and screenshots.

Reminder and supply views are for personal planning only, not dose instructions.

What GLPzy does not do: GLPzy does not prescribe medicine, recommend doses, diagnose symptoms, change treatment plans, replace official medicine information or replace advice from a qualified clinician.

Safety boundary: Estimated Exposure is a personal tracking estimate, not measured blood concentration and not medical advice. Do not use Estimated Exposure to guide dosing. Always check with your clinician before making medical decisions.
