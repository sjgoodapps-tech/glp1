FAQ

Questions before downloading

What is GLPzy?

GLPzy is an iPhone app for tracking GLP-1 routines. It helps you record doses, weight, symptoms, nutrition, reminders, photos, imports, exports and optional read-only Apple Health context.

Do I need an account?

No in-app account is required to get started.

Does GLPzy work with Apple Health?

Yes, optionally. GLPzy can read weight history, height, glucose readings, body composition, movement, workouts, calories, protein, water, carbs, fat, fiber and sugar from Apple Health. It does not write data back to Apple Health.

Can I track custom or compounded treatments?

Yes. GLPzy includes supported medication selections, plus custom and compounded profiles for personal tracking.

What stays free?

Free includes setup, daily dose entry, reminders, the Today dashboard, core history, current and previous month calendar review, core charts, historical Estimated Exposure context, optional read-only Apple Health context, CSV import, CSV/JSON export for core records, photo comparison and the small Next Dose widget. Basic same-treatment edits stay free: dose, start date, and reminder time.

What does Premium add?

Premium adds broader older and future calendar browsing, projected Estimated Exposure scenarios, advanced summaries, clinician-ready PDF summaries, deeper export/reporting tools, injection-site rotation review, maintenance tools, larger widgets, Apple Watch support and longer-range planning tools. Advanced treatment changes may require Premium.

Where can I find the app's medical references?

GLPzy includes in-app medical references from Settings and directly from BMI and Estimated Exposure Trend source buttons, with matching bibliography pages on this site.

Is GLPzy medical advice?

No. GLPzy is for personal tracking and review. Estimated Exposure Trend is a relative personal-tracking estimate. It is not a measured blood concentration. It is based on a simplified model. Do not use this for dosing decisions. Do not use Estimated Exposure to guide dosing. Always check with your clinician before making medical decisions.

Can I read the policy pages before using the app?

Yes. The site includes public pages for privacy, support, legal and trader information, data rights, medical safety, and methodology.
