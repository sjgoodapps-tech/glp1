  1. Record dose dates, reminder times and injection sites.
  2. Add weight, symptom, appetite, nutrition, note and photo context when useful.

What GLPzy does

  • keeps Zepbound context in a private record
  • supports injection-site and reminder review

What GLPzy does not do

  • provide Zepbound dosing advice
  • interpret side effects
