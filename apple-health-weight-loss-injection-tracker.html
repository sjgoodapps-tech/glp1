FAQ

Questions about Apple Health

Does GLPzy write data to Apple Health?

No. GLPzy's Apple Health support is optional and read-only. It does not write data back to Apple Health.

What Apple Health context can GLPzy use?

Where permission is granted, GLPzy can use read-only weight history, height, glucose readings, body composition, movement, workouts and nutrition context.

Do I need Apple Health to use GLPzy?

No. Apple Health support is optional. GLPzy can still be used with manual tracking.
