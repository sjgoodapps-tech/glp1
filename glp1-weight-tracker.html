Questions about weight tracking

Can GLPzy track weight with GLP-1 doses?

Yes. GLPzy can track weight history alongside doses, symptoms, notes, nutrition and progress photos.

Does GLPzy work with Apple Health weight data?

Yes. Apple Health support is optional and read-only, and can add weight history and other permitted context.

Does GLPzy set weight goals for me?

No. GLPzy is for personal tracking and review. It does not provide medical advice or set clinical goals.
