GLP-1 weight tracker app for iPhone

GLPzy helps you track weight history beside GLP-1 dose records, symptoms, appetite, nutrition and progress photos. Optional Apple Health support can add read-only weight context. GLPzy is for personal review and does not judge progress or give treatment advice.

How it works

  1. Record weight manually or use optional read-only Apple Health context.
  2. Review weight beside dose stages, symptoms, appetite, nutrition and photos.

What GLPzy does

  • tracks manual weight records
  • can read Apple Health weight context with permission

What GLPzy does not do

  • set clinical goals
  • judge progress
  • tell you to change treatment
  • write data back to Apple Health

FAQ

Common questions

Can I track weight in GLPzy?

Yes. You can track weight history beside dose and symptom records.

Can Apple Health add weight data?

Apple Health support is optional and read-only. With permission, GLPzy can read weight, height, glucose, body composition, movement, workouts and nutrition context. GLPzy does not write data back to Apple Health.

Does GLPzy judge my progress?

No. GLPzy records and shows your data for personal review.

Can I export weight records?

CSV and JSON for core records. Premium adds deeper summaries and clinician-ready PDF summaries.

Can I review weight with photos?

Yes. Weight and photo history can be reviewed together.

Does GLPzy set clinical goals?

No. It does not set clinical goals or give treatment advice.
