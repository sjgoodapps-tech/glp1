Direct answer

Tirzepatide tracker app for iPhone

GLPzy is a private tirzepatide tracker for iPhone and iPad. It can record dose dates, reminder times, injection sites, weight, symptoms, notes, photos and optional read-only Apple Health context. It is for personal review only and does not guide dosing.

Get GLPzy

Tirzepatide is the ingredient used in some GLP-1 and GIP medicine contexts. GLPzy supports personal tracking records without making treatment claims.

Use the app to review your own routine, not to decide whether to start, stop, skip, delay or change a dose.

How it works

  1. Choose the medicine context you want to record.
  2. Log dose, site, reminder, symptom, weight and photo records.
  3. Review related Mounjaro and Zepbound pages for brand-context tracking notes.

What GLPzy does

  • tracks tirzepatide context as personal data
  • links brand pages and ingredient context
  • supports export for user records

What GLPzy does not do

  • provide treatment instructions
  • recommend dose changes
  • measure medicine levels

Safety boundary: Estimated Exposure is a personal tracking estimate, not measured blood concentration and not medical advice. Do not use Estimated Exposure to guide dosing. Always check with your clinician before making medical decisions. Read the medical safety page.
