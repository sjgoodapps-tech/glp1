FAQ

Questions people are likely to ask before downloading

What is OneGLP?

OneGLP is an iPhone and iPad app for tracking GLP-1 routines. It helps you record doses, weight, symptoms, reminders, and routine history in one place.

Do I need an account?

No in-app account is required to get started.

Does OneGLP work with Apple Health?

Yes, optionally. OneGLP can read weight history, height, body composition, movement, workouts, calories, protein, and water summaries from Apple Health in read-only mode for Today, Insights, and Settings.

Can I track custom or compounded treatments?

Yes. OneGLP includes supported medication selections, plus custom and compounded profiles for personal tracking.

What stays free?

Free includes medication setup, treatment edits, daily dose entry, reminders, the Today dashboard, core history, current and previous month calendar review, core charts, historical Estimated Exposure context, Apple Health import and context, CSV import, photo comparison and sharing, and a small Next Dose widget.

What does Premium add?

Premium adds older and future calendar browsing, projected Estimated Exposure scenarios, a premium exposure review screen, advanced summaries, CSV/JSON/PDF export, PDF summaries for your clinician, injection-site rotation review, supply tracking, maintenance tools, larger widgets, Apple Watch support, and longer-range planning tools.

Where can I find the app's medical references?

OneGLP includes in-app medical references from Settings and directly from BMI and Estimated Exposure Trend source buttons, with matching bibliography pages on this site.

Is OneGLP medical advice?

No. OneGLP is for personal tracking and review. Estimated Exposure Trend is a relative personal-tracking estimate. It is not a measured blood concentration. It is based on a simplified model. Do not use this for dosing decisions. Do not use Estimated Exposure to guide dosing. Always check with your clinician before making medical decisions.

Can I read the policy pages before using the app?

Yes. The site includes public pages for privacy, support, legal and trader information, data rights, medical safety, and methodology.
