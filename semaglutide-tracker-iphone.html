FAQ

Questions about tracking semaglutide in GLPzy

Can I use GLPzy as a semaglutide tracker?

Yes. GLPzy can track semaglutide routines with dose logs, reminders, weight, symptoms, notes, photos and review history for personal tracking.

Which medicine pages connect to semaglutide?

The Wegovy tracker page links back to this broader semaglutide page for users who want an ingredient-level view.

Does GLPzy work with Apple Health?

Yes. Apple Health support is optional and read-only, and can add context such as weight, glucose, body, movement and nutrition data where permission is granted.

Does GLPzy provide dosing advice?

No. GLPzy is a personal tracking and review app only. It does not provide dosing advice.
