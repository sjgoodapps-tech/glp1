Direct answer

Private GLP-1 tracker with no account required

GLPzy is a local-first GLP-1 tracker. Core tracking works without a mandatory in-app account. Your records are kept on your device unless you export, share or restore a local backup. Optional Apple Health support is read-only and does not write data back.

Get GLPzy

You do not need to create an in-app account to record doses, weight, symptoms, reminders or photos.

Exports and local backups are user-directed. You choose when to create a file, share it or restore it.

How it works

  1. Record core tracking data on the device.
  2. Export or back up records only when you choose.
  3. Review privacy, data rights and support pages for the policy details.

What GLPzy does

  • keeps core tracking local-first
  • supports user-directed exports and local backups
  • keeps Apple Health optional and read-only

What GLPzy does not do

  • require a mandatory in-app account for core tracking
  • create public profiles
  • write data back to Apple Health

Safety boundary: Estimated Exposure is a personal tracking estimate, not measured blood concentration and not medical advice. Do not use Estimated Exposure to guide dosing. Always check with your clinician before making medical decisions. Read the medical safety page.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account to use GLPzy?

No mandatory in-app account is required for core tracking.

Where are my records kept?

Local-first. Records stay on the device unless the user exports, shares or restores a local backup.

Does Apple Health change that?

No. Apple Health support is optional and read-only.

Can I export my records?

CSV and JSON for core records. Premium adds deeper summaries and clinician-ready PDF summaries.

Can I restore a backup?

Local backup and restore are user-directed tools.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

Use the privacy, data rights and support pages linked on this site.
